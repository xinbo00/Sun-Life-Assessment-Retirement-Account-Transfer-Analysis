
# Retirement Account Transfer Analysis | SQL & Tableau

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retirement account transfer outcomes, including rollovers, withdrawals, and no-movement cases. It focuses on understanding account holder behavior, evaluating retention patterns, and identifying opportunities to improve customer engagement.

The analysis covers 199,885 transfer records representing approximately $15.1 billion in termination balances.

## Tools & Technologies

- **SQL:** Data exploration, cleaning, transformation, and aggregation
- **Tableau:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- **Excel:** Data validation and analytical exports
- **Business Analytics:** Customer segmentation, KPI development, and retention analysis

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Exploration
Explored retirement account transfer records to understand data structure, completeness, distributions, and potential data quality issues.

### 2. Data Cleaning & Transformation
Developed SQL queries to clean and transform financial records, standardize date and financial fields, and prepare datasets for analysis.

### 3. KPI Development
Created analytical tables and metrics to evaluate:
- Rollover rates
- Withdrawal rates
- Retention ratios
- Account balance tiers
- Engagement channels
- Contact timing

### 4. Tableau Dashboard
Developed interactive Tableau visualizations to explore transfer outcomes, retention patterns, and customer segments.

### 5. Business Insights
Investigated potential retention opportunities by balance tier, age group, engagement channel, and contact timing.

The project highlights the importance of proactive engagement, timely follow-up, and coordinated digital and advisor outreach.
